Namaste, I’m Devansh

Most of us grew up following rituals and customs because our families did, and because it never felt right to ask why. I make videos for that moment of curiosity: where did this come from, and what is the reality behind it?

My work is documentaries and explainers on local traditions and riti-riwaaj, read through ancient scriptures and careful research. I’m not here to mock anyone’s faith or tell you what to believe. I want to give you the full story, so the choice stays yours.

Reality is just in front of you. It needs to be recovered.The idea behind the channel

How I approach every story

Respect for people

I question ideas, never the people who follow them. Every tradition belongs to a family that cares about it.

Sources before opinions

Scriptures, records and local voices come first. If I can’t back something up, I don’t say it.

Plain language

No jargon and no lectures. If a grandparent and a student can’t both follow it, I haven’t done my job.

Open to correction

If I get something wrong, tell me. Learning in public is part of the work.

What I cover

Rituals and riti-riwaaj

Local customs from homes, villages and festivals: what is actually done, where it comes from, and what it was meant to achieve.

Beliefs, questioned

Old beliefs that get repeated without explanation. I look at them calmly, with evidence, and without mocking anyone’s faith.

The scripture lens

What the ancient texts actually say compared with what is commonly said they say, and where the two have drifted apart.

Documentaries and explainers

Long-form documentaries for the full story, and shorter informative videos to spread awareness quickly.

How every video is made

  1. Notice the practice

    Pick a ritual or tradition people follow without a second thought.

  2. Trace it back

    Go to the sources and the scriptures to find where it started and what changed.

  3. Say it plainly

    Share what I found in simple language so anyone can understand it.
